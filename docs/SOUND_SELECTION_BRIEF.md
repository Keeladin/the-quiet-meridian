# Sound choices for The Quiet Meridian

Codex now handles routine recording selection, editing, timing, loops,
variation, volume and integration. You can judge the overall atmosphere as
you play and request changes; you do not need to audition every effect.
Your opening choices remain in use. File descriptions and audio analysis guide
further selections; subjective listening approval is not implied by those checks.
A filename's category need not dictate how the recording is used.

The game should make generous use of the library: distinct room atmospheres,
material details, moving mechanisms, character movement and story payoffs.
There is no fixed ceiling on the number of sounds. The batches below organise
the choices so work can proceed one setting at a time.

For an optional override, send the cue name and filename, for example **Harbour water — filename.wav**.
Several favourite files for the same cue are welcome; they can become variations
or separate layers. Include the folder name when filenames are ambiguous.
You can pick from any of your three versions. Codex will locate the matching
source and handle the playback format. The thirteen opening-room choices have been received and integrated in the
isolated preview and full campaign. Twenty further masters now support
36 room atmospheres and selected arrival/mechanism cues. Additional cue
families remain available when a scene benefits from them.
See `audio-selection/requests.json` for exact filenames and status.

## Story and puzzle payoffs

These cues give discoveries weight. Choose recordings that fit the grounded
mystery and its changing emotions. They may be musical, physical or a deliberate
combination, depending on the moment.

| Cue | Where and when it plays | What the sound should represent | Playback and choices |
|---|---|---|---|
| Components fit | The player assembles a useful tool or repairs something | Pieces physically fitting and a clear sense of completion | Material-specific fitting sounds plus an optional completion accent |
| Puzzle resolved | A room problem is solved | Understanding followed by a tangible result | Several payoff choices, with the actual mechanism carrying its own sound |
| Important evidence revealed | A discovery changes the interpretation of the case | Intrigue, significance and a reason to look more closely | Several reveal accents suited to different levels of importance |
| New route opened | A newly usable area or passage becomes available | The world opening up and an invitation to explore | A transition accent supported by the actual gate/door/environment change |
| Chapter resolved | A chapter's smaller story reaches its conclusion | An earned, memorable resolution with momentum into the next chapter | Distinct chapter payoffs where the story benefits from them |
| Tension rises | A scripted threat, contradiction or reversal changes the mood | Anticipation and uncertainty appropriate to a grounded mystery | Several atmosphere shifts or accents, timed to authored moments |
| Final resolution | The overall mystery is resolved and the epilogue begins | Emotional release, consequence and a sense of completion | A distinctive final payoff that can work with the score |

## Turning choices into the game's soundscape

The owner selects the recordings. Codex records those choices, preserves their
source identities, prepares the required variations and mixes them in context.
Choices can be revised after hearing them during actual play.

There can be many sounds across a location without every sound playing at
once. Distance, timing, material, current mechanism state and the story determine
what is heard. Important clue sounds remain readable, and dialogue and player
volume controls remain effective. Ambient details must not accidentally mimic
a puzzle signal or reveal an unfound object.

The current preview has basic discovery audio and music. This brief describes
the richer next implementation; the requested layers and states are not already
wired into the game. Chapter grouping and search rewards are tracked separately
in `CHAPTER_SEARCH_STRUCTURE.md`.
