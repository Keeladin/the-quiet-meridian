# The Quiet Meridian

Creative and narrative plan, 14 November 2026. Original fiction created for this
repository. Spoilers throughout. Production target remains 6–10 hours; no elapsed
playtime is implied by these plans or by automated solution traversal.

## Promise

In the tidal city of Leth, bells tell people when they may cross the streets.
Seventeen years ago the East Reach drowned between two bells. The official
record calls it an unprecedented tide and blames a dead lockkeeper. Now the
archivist who found a second record has disappeared.

Play Iona Vale, a paper conservator returning to the city that made her father's
name a warning. Restore physical evidence, reconstruct machines and timelines,
listen to people who survived, and assemble a public case that cannot simply be
removed from a shelf. The mystery has a definite human resolution. There are no
supernatural causes, dream reversals, random passwords or timed failure states.

The title refers both to the observatory's unstruck midnight signal and to the
line the city drew between people it recorded and people it chose to forget.

## Tone and visual identity

Painterly maritime architecture, oxidized teal, indigo water, ochre lamplight,
ivory paper and restrained vermilion evidence marks. Handworked brass, salt,
cloth and glass. Begin under rain; end with clear dawn. Grounded technology:
mechanical clocks, photographic plates, pneumatic dispatch and tidal works;
an invented late-industrial period rather than a specific historical nation.
The city's inhabitants are people with ordinary loyalties, shame and humor.
Violence remains historical and non-graphic. No jump scares.

## Cast and dramatic arcs

* **Iona Vale**, protagonist and conservator: precise, impatient with easy
  stories. Her father Tomas operated the East Reach gates. She begins wanting
  one innocent name restored; she ends making room for every omitted name.
* **Mara Venn**, municipal archivist: presumed missing, actually sheltering in
  the old island refuge. She preserved a duplicate but refused to publish an
  allegation that would erase the people inside it. Her secrecy is consequential
  and understandable, not presented as a universally wise choice.
* **Rowan Pike**, lockkeeper: kept Tomas's tool roll and lied about doing so.
  His shame is that he obeyed a closure order instead of waking the quay.
* **Nadi Bell**, ferryperson: carried survivors and remembers routes rather
  than dates. She later carries witnesses and records in separate boats.
* **Sera Quill**, printer: her mother's petition was seized before the flood.
  She makes publication distributed and durable, after checking every claim.
* **Elian Ash**, engineer: signed the diversion order under Rusk's threat of
  losing the works. He believed a forged evacuation return. His admission
  helps prove the case but does not erase his responsibility.
* **Ada Flint**, retired astronomer: a clock discrepancy made her doubt the
  official chronology. Her plate archive supplies an independent time source.
* **Dorian Rusk**, magistrate: ordered a controlled diversion to destroy a
  petition and protect the foundry land transaction. He knew the return was
  false. Twenty-three people died; the flood damaged more than he intended.
  He then altered records and blamed Tomas. He is removed and referred for
  trial; the ending does not pretend a hearing is itself a trial.
* **Tomas Vale**, deceased gatekeeper: refused to open the residential sluice.
  His refusal was bypassed at the crown. His last action was ringing the small
  handbell and opening the upper footway. The investigation finds that act;
  it does not invent a final letter conveniently explaining everything.

## Complete act structure

1. **The Borrowed Bell** — Arrival, missing archivist, torn dispatch and two
   contradictory tide records. Learn examination, evidence, combining, codes
   and reconstruction. The archive seal proves Mara left voluntarily after
   finding the removed folio. Hook: the flood began before the tide turned.
2. **Water Has a Memory** — Reconstruct the works from physical wear, valves,
   shift meals and pressure traces. Rowan admits obeying the closure. Discover
   the diversion used a council bypass, not Tomas's residential gate. The
   preserved pressure chart leads to the observatory's silent time signal.
3. **The Unstruck Hour** — Reconcile local clocks, photographic exposure,
   rooftop signals and a stopped maintenance clock. Ada's plates establish
   00:14 as the deliberate opening, while the alleged evacuation return was
   sent at 00:22. Mara used the same signal route toward the island.
4. **Names in the Reeds** — Follow a safe marsh route; recover the clinic
   register and records of unnamed lodgers. Mara is found alive. Establish the
   twenty-three victims, distinguish a real evacuation plan from a false return,
   and preserve her original tracing. Investigation shifts from private
   vindication to a case in which survivors can speak for themselves.
5. **The Price of Silence** — Follow deeds, accounts, type impressions and
   dispatch custody. Rusk's material interest and instruction are proven
   independently of Elian's confession. Elian admits his act; Sera prepares
   multiple accurate copies. Rusk orders the old records burned as redundant.
   Countermeasure is lawful public deposition before the posted deadline,
   represented without real-time pressure.
6. **A City That Remembers** — Protect the record through independent copies,
   witness order, safe gate locking and authenticated relays. Present a precise
   account: act, time, authority, intent, consequence. Rusk's tide explanation
   fails against records from three independent systems. Council removes his
   emergency authority and refers the case to an outside court. The old flood
   register is corrected in a final room where every victim is named.

## Resolution and epilogue

Mara returns to an archive with public duplicate holdings. Rowan's testimony
is recorded beside his failure. Elian submits to the same inquiry as Rusk;
there is no pardon bestowed by the player. Nadi's ferry route is renamed for
the people it saved, not the investigator. Sera prints the corrected register
at cost. Ada's midnight signal sounds again. Iona corrects her father's entry
and stays long enough to teach two apprentices how to repair paper without
hiding the repair. A visible seam is the final image and the game's governing
metaphor: repair is possible without pretending the damage never happened.

## Search-driven chapter structure

The owner has clarified that each chapter must function as an exciting smaller
story within the overall mystery. Several map areas contain a couple of
hidden-object rooms each. Completing a search awards a clue or item that feeds
a room solution or chapter deduction. See `CHAPTER_SEARCH_STRUCTURE.md` for
area groups, proposed rewards, chapter questions and resolutions. The existing
six-chapter plot remains the narrative baseline; its old ten-challenge-per-
chapter allocation describes the alpha, not a required workload for the redesign.

## Location and challenge allocation

The current alpha contains six revisitable environments and ten challenges
per chapter. Its finales require the other nine challenges plus the prior
chapter final where applicable. The search-driven replacement must follow
the new reward/deduction structure rather than preserve those counts blindly. Internal branches permit exploration in more
than one order. Wrong solutions never consume items, and no conversation choice
can destroy evidence. All essential clue sources remain available.

## Pacing budget and acceptance

Target chapter budgets are 55–85, 60–90, 65–95, 65–95, 65–95 and 50–80
minutes, for 6–9 hours before optional reading. These are design budgets, not
measurements. Each has an exploration pass, parallel investigations, a physical
reconstruction and a synthesis. The intended solution is never to hunt pixels
without accessible labels, guess an arbitrary number or wait for a timer.

The authored minimum is not sufficient evidence of length. Independent first-time
playtests must record solve time, reading time, hint use and abandoned hypotheses.
If the build is shorter, deepen evidence interactions and spatial/mechanical
puzzles; do not pad with delays, repeated object lists or redundant backtracking.

## Known content-design limits to validate

A data-driven text interaction can convey a coherent case without delivering the
full physical richness of a polished adventure. Every clue location needs a
review against its actual painting. Hidden-object scenes need legible objects,
not only clickable labels. Dialogue should be tested for reveal order across
free exploration. The final dependency graph prevents premature chapter closure,
but an early inspectable document can still spoil a later deduction unless its
wording is careful. A designer solution traversal is not a first-time playtest.
