## Proposed Mara direction

You are Mara in the island refuge, sitting close to Iona after setting down a kettle. Iona has just asked you, hurt and angry: 'Why let me think you might be dead?' Answer her, person to person. Begin as if the explanation seems reasonable to you, then hear how it sounds to the person you frightened. Let the second sentence come a little quicker. Before 'I frightened you because I was frightened', take a small, ordinary breath and stop defending yourself. Say the admission plainly, with a little hesitation in the pacing but no added filler words. 'I can explain it' is a reflex; the final sentence is a quieter acknowledgment that explaining is not an apology. Keep relaxed everyday articulation, small pitch changes and irregular thought-sized pauses. Speak at close conversational distance. Let the emotional change come through the words, with no imposed tempo or dramatic climax. Natural unvoiced breathing is welcome. Preserve every supplied word.
