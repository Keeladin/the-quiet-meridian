Reviewed 14 November 2026 from preserved generated PNGs using chapter contact
sheets. All eighteen scenes are distinct, share the teal/amber maritime visual
language, and contain no people, interface, logos or watermarks. The dawn shift
in chapter six is visible. Dimensions and hashes are in
`assets/art/manifest-ch4-6.json`; exact prompts are in `docs/art-prompts/`.

`content/hotspot-layout-ch4-6.json` recommends normalized interaction centers on
the depicted desks, records, markers, machinery and testimony positions. The
recommendations are an interaction-placement pass, not proof that every named
evidence object has been painted literally. Runtime object icons and accessible
labels must remain available. Avoid claiming these paintings alone supply the
textual answers.

Specific limitations:

* Reed Jetty prose now describes the pictured rust-coloured cloth and uses
  Cloth route tag / Crossing marker labels. The written route concerns a willow
  crossing elsewhere, without claiming a willow tree is visible in this scene.
  Colour recognition is never required.
* Clinic records, glasshouse labels, survey drawings, deeds, notices, accounts,
  dispatch receipts and relay instructions are environmental shapes. The
  readable contents live in the evidence panels. The clinic triage board and
  some individual loose sheets are not separately identifiable in the painting.
* Memorial Garden provides a representative group of unlettered markers; it
  does not visually enumerate twenty-three victims or their category counts.
  The inspected records supply the counts. p37 explicitly tells players to use
  the transcribed names rather than count the painted stones.
* Testimony interactions mark a place or document associated with a speaker.
  The environment paintings are empty; portraits/dialogue supply the people.
* Gate Crown has credible isolation machinery and a red safety lever, but the
  exact collectible pin and signed tag require the overlaid item art.
* The memory-room register is deliberately blank in the painting; its repaired
  seam and archival setting establish the ending, and the evidence panel gives
  the actual correction. Three evidence positions are close to the same desk;
  label mode needs overlap review at the minimum supported window size.
