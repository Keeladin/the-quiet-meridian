## Direction

Preserve the original engraved, painterly teal/ochre world. Movement should
make Leth feel inhabited: surface water and drifting mist outside; light,
steam, paper dust and working mechanisms inside. Historical evidence clocks
and safety-critical gate levers must not move merely as decoration. Paint new
character expressions from the existing fictional cast rather than borrowing
an actor's likeness. Use quiet chamber themes that develop through a chapter;
reserve tension and resolution arrangements for deductions and chapter ends.

## Acceptance that is deliberately still open

The presentation reference is an ambition, not a claim that this build matches
Grim Legends or Enigmatis. Complete voice production, listening-based dialogue
mix review, all-scene art/motion review, native Windows gameplay, full manual
playthrough and blind 6–10 hour duration tests are required. Recorded synthetic
tones in tests establish playback behavior only; they are never game dialogue.
No new runtime account, service or internet dependency is introduced.

delivery. One separately approved Mara revision is captured and independently

US$0.25 cap. All current take approvals are used. Full production is still gated
on listening acceptance and a new spending authorization.
